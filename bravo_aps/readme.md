**ACTIVE PROTECTION SYSTEM**

**USER MANUAL**

The active protection system attempts to intercept shells, missiles, and rockets that are about to hit the vehicle.
APS can be added to a vehicle by scripting (bravo_fnc_apsInit) or by using the Zeus module provided. Note that the script function must be run on all clients with JIP.

The APS starts in the disarmed state. It can be armed, and then disarmed again, by the vehicle commander or driver from the action menu (if ACE is not loaded) or the ACE interaction menu.

The APS contains a single interception charge. After firing, it can be reloaded from outside the vehicle if you have a replacement charge in your inventory. You can check if the APS is loaded by disarming and arming it - the system indicates "NO CHARGE" in the HUD notification for arming if the APS is not loaded.

The APS will provide an auditory alert to the vehicle crew when it attempts an interception, as well as a visual warning to crewmembers looking at their optics.

The maximum interception range is 50 metres.

**THE APS IS DANGEROUS**. Intercepting a projectile creates an explosion which can injure or kill nearby units.

**THE APS IS NOT A PERFECT SHIELD**. It may struggle to intercept high-velocity projectiles or projectiles with unusual trajectories. Its efficiency also depends on the shooter's frame rate. Low frame rates on the shooter's machine can reduce the APS's maximum interception velocity.
